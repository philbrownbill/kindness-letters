FOR PARTICIPATING ORGANISATIONS

A little care goes a long way.

Thank you for helping to share Kindness Letters in Cheddar and the surrounding Somerset villages. These simple guidelines help keep the project welcoming and thoughtful for everyone.

Before you take part

Agree participation with the project team first. We’ll discuss the size of your batch, collection or delivery, and how you’d like to share letters.

Writing letters

  • Keep messages kind, inclusive and encouraging. A cheerful story or drawing is welcome.
  • Leave out names, addresses, contact details and other identifying information.
  • Do not include requests for money, promotional material, links or requests for a reply.
  • Leave letters unsealed so our volunteers can check them before distribution.
  • Follow your organisation’s usual safeguarding practices when organising a writing activity.

Receiving an official batch

Each official batch is confirmed directly with your organisation before delivery. Our volunteers check letters before sealing them. A sticker or label on its own does not prove that a letter is genuine.

If a delivery is unexpected or you are unsure about it, set it aside and check with your established project contact before sharing any letters.

Sharing letters

You decide whether and how to offer letters. People may take one if they wish; there is no pressure, no reply expected and no obligation to continue participating.

For care settings or other supported groups, use your usual judgement about suitability and individual needs. If a letter causes concern, stop offering it and contact the project team.

Keeping it simple

There are no fees or fundraising requests. You do not need to collect recipients’ names or contact details for this project.

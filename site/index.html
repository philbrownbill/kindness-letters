PUT PEN TO PAPER

Help us spread
a little kindness.

Get your class, group or family together and write some Kindness Letters. It’s a lovely way to think about kindness and how a few words can brighten someone’s day.

For schools, Cubs, Scouts, families and community groups in Cheddar and the surrounding villages.

We’d like to write letters

It’s completely free to take part. Ordinary paper, pens and a little kindness are all you need.

  1. Get together.

    Organise a letter-writing activity with your class, group or family.

  2. Write something kind.

    A positive message, a funny story, an encouraging thought or a cheerful drawing.

  3. Keep it anonymous.

    Don’t include personal details, addresses or contact information.

  4. Get in touch.

    We’ll agree how to collect or receive your letters, check them and pass them on.

MAKE ROOM FOR KINDNESS

Could you help us share a little kindness?

We’re looking for friendly community spaces in Cheddar and the surrounding villages where people can discover a Kindness Letter.

Place a small box of letters on your counter, or offer them directly to people you support. Cafés, libraries, community centres, food banks and care homes are all welcome.

  1. Get in touch. Let us know you’d like to take part.
  2. Receive your letters. We’ll arrange a small batch of checked and sealed letters.
  3. Share a little kindness. Display them or offer them to someone who might appreciate one.

ROOTED IN OUR COMMUNITY

Kindness connects us all.

Kindness Letters is a small, volunteer-led initiative based in Cheddar, Somerset. It started with a simple thought: what if we could make someone’s day a little brighter, just by taking a few minutes to write them a letter?

We believe kindness doesn’t have to be complicated or expensive. Sometimes a few thoughtful words are enough.

This Christmas, we’re inviting families, schools and community organisations across Cheddar and the surrounding villages to help write and share letters close to home.

Our aim is simple: a few more moments of connection between neighbours, across Cheddar and the villages around us.
